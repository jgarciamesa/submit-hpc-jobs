# The Carpentries Code of Conduct

*Please do read this whole thing. It's not that long and it will make you feel
better.*

The Carpentries (which includes *The Carpentry*, *Software Carpentry*, and
*Data Carpentry*) are dedicated to providing an welcoming learning environment
for everyone.

We expect all participants at The Carpentries events to follow these rules:

1. Participants must be tolerant of viewpoints different from their own and
   respectful of other participants and of the staff running the event.
2. Participants must be respectful of the time of other participants and of
   staff running the event.
3. Sexual or other gender-based harassment and discrimination are not allowed.
   Same for race, religion, nationality, body size, political orientation, or
   any other form of harassment or discrimination.
4. Participants must accept criticism in a civil manner.
5. Side conversations should be kept to a minimum, and in any case only when
   they cannot be had elsewhere.

These rules apply to all The Carpentries spaces, including workshops,
conferences, mailing lists, forums (online and in person), and on social media.
For example, using a person's GitHub username to find their email address in
order to contact them without first asking for permission is not allowed.

Instructors and workshop organizers have the right and responsibility to
remove any materials in any media that reflect allowed behaviour. Instructors
and workshop organizers will address, in a civil manner, incident
behaviour. Participants asked to stop a behaviour must do so immediately or
will be asked to leave the event by workshop organizers (this decision will be
final: appealing will not be permitted). We will not provide details about the
incident.

If you are the target of unwanted behaviour, or observe something that makes
you uncomfortable, or in any other way have any concerns, please contact a
workshop organizer as soon as possible. We will take seriously all
complaints, keep them confidential, and investigate them as far as possible.

We will not tolerate retaliation by someone in authority against a person who
reports an incident or behaviour. We consider such behaviour itself to be
an incident and will deal with it as such.
